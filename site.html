The process

Every signal. One saliva sample.

Two halves, one chain of custody. Your staff own the first four minutes; we own everything after the cooler closes. Nothing in between needs a phlebotomist, a sterile room or a change to your existing setup.

In the changing room · ~4 min In our lab · 48 h to report
  1. 0160s saliva sample A swab in the mouth for one minute. Zero needles, no phlebotomist, and no medical staff required on site.
  2. 02Name selection Pick the athlete in the app, so the sample is attributed at the point of collection rather than reconciled later.
  3. 03Barcode scan Scan the tube to bind it to the session record before it leaves the room.
  4. 04Store cooled Into the cooler until pickup. That is the last thing anyone at the club has to do.
  5. 05Swab delivery & pickup Consumables out, samples back. Logistics are part of the platform, not a separate contract you have to manage.
  6. 06LC–MS/MS readout 1 ml of saliva is ionised and sorted by mass-to-charge ratio, producing a spectral fingerprint for every biomarker at once.
  7. 07Targeted quantification Discovery work is untargeted and literature-led; in production the targeted assay runs, quantified against known standards.
  8. 08Report in the dashboard Results land against each athlete’s own baseline, with trend and outlier flags already computed.
Because collection is effortless, testing can happen daily — and daily is what turns a single reading into a curve you can act on.

The science behind the product

Turning Spit Into Spectra.

Mass spectrometry ionises the molecules in a saliva sample and sorts them by their mass-to-charge ratio, producing a unique spectral fingerprint for every biomarker.

Breakthrough technology

Leveraging mass spectrometry for highly sensitive analyses to eliminate tedious blood draws for daily testing.

Privacy

ISO 27001 compliant and highly secured processes and servers ensure privacy and data safety.

Scalability

Proprietary processes and AI bring high throughput testing to the masses.

Saliva against blood

One medium beats the other on everything except habit.

Blood is not an ideal diagnostic medium for 99% of cases. It is invasive, which limits how frequently samples can be taken — and without consistent monitoring, true health and wellbeing remain elusive.

About us

Not just a simple lab.

We believe that the future of health isn’t reactive — it’s proactive.

Our motivation

Shaping diagnostics for the next decade.

At Biolyz, we stand for uncompromising precision. In a world full of guesswork, we’re here to deliver clarity and certainty. Health should never be left to chance.

Blood is simply not an ideal diagnostic medium for 99% of cases. It’s invasive, which limits how frequently samples can be taken. Without consistent monitoring, true health and wellbeing remain elusive.

We’re unlocking every body’s data, effortlessly revealing the inner insights that empower elite athletes, proactive health seekers, and communities around the globe.

Our technology translates complex, hard science into clear, immediate, and humane insights, so you can understand your body better, faster, and more naturally.

We champion access and equality in advanced diagnostics, ensuring everyone — everywhere — can take proactive steps toward their optimal performance and well-being. We know data only matters if it improves real lives.

This is the era of clarity. This is the age of precision. This is Biolyz.

Manifesto

We need to accelerate first principle prognostics.

The world still runs on 20th century diagnostics: invasive, expensive, episodic, reactive. Between doctor visits, humanity flies blind.

That ends with saliva.

A single drop carries the same hormones, metabolites, and proteins doctors chase in blood — without the needle, the clinic, or the cost. The signal was always there. The world just lacked the tools to read it.

Our LC-MS platform measures it at scale: high throughput, multiplexed, noninvasive, and orders of magnitude cheaper than blood.

That changes what diagnostics can be. Not an annual snapshot, but a continuous feed. Not reacting to disease, but reading health as it happens.

Where others see saliva, we see signal.

From reactive to proactive

Our master plan is three-tiered, ruthless in logic, clear in execution.

  1. Tier 01Elite teams and individuals We start with elite athlete teams and individuals living at the edge of biological performance. Painless daily monitoring of the molecules that drive strength, recovery, inflammation, and hormone balance.
  2. Tier 02Vanguard individuals Next, the rising wave of high performance individuals — athletes, biohackers, longevity pioneers. People who want radical ownership of their biology, the early adopters of the post sickcare age.
  3. Tier 03Humanity Finally, everyone else. Schools, clinics, families — entire populations monitored weekly, not once a year. Preventive diagnostics at planetary scale: a new kind of healthcare infrastructure.
Technology and approach

We didn’t pivot into this space. We engineered our way in.

Our platform isn’t a patchwork of commodity kits — it’s a vertically integrated diagnostic engine with the precision of a lab and the scale of software. We’re building the substrate personalized health will run on.

This is not a company. It’s a movement.

We’re recruiting missionaries, not mercenaries — scientists, engineers, operators, and rebels who see biology as the next frontier of intelligence and want to build the rails. Especially in the rising wake of artificial intelligence.

This isn’t a bet on another wellness app. It’s infrastructure for the biological internet, and we intend to be the architects of a new medical epoch.

What the policy has to cover

  • Categories of personal data, including health data under Art. 9 GDPR, and the separate legal basis that special category processing requires.
  • Purposes and legal basis per purpose — contract, consent, legitimate interest — kept apart rather than bundled.
  • Who the data is shared with: the club, the laboratory, sub-processors, and what each one may see.
  • Retention periods, and what happens to an athlete’s data when they leave the club.
  • Data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and how to exercise each.
  • International transfers, and the mechanism relied on for each.
  • Automated processing and profiling, given the platform produces scores and recommendations.
